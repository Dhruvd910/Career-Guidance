# Phase 2 hands-on test — memory

Three short sittings, ideally on different days (at least 6 hours apart between B and C, so MAYA
treats it as a return). Use MAYA on the Pi as a student would. Tell Claude "done" after each
sitting if you like; it reads what MAYA stored and checks it against what you said.

## A. Permissions (5 minutes)

1. On MAYA's page, tap **"MAYA can remember your conversations — set it up"** (or menu →
   MAYA's memory → Permissions).
2. Read the notice; tap **हिंदी में पढ़ें** and back.
3. Tick **Remember our conversations** and **Notice how I'm feeling**, tap Save *without* the
   guardian details → it should refuse and say what's missing.
4. Fill in the guardian's name, relationship and phone, tick the agreement, Save → "Saved."

## B. First conversation (10 minutes)

Talk about a real-feeling dilemma, in the language you'd naturally use. For example:

- "Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha ki class 11 mein PCM lu ya PCB."
- Say what you like (a subject, something you made), and one constraint ("I don't want to leave
  my city", "my parents want me to be a doctor").
- Agree to some next step she suggests.

Then leave MAYA alone for 10 minutes (or close her). Open **MAYA's memory → What she
remembers**: the topic, your interests, the constraint (marked *Private* if it's about family or
money), and a summary under *Recent conversations* should be there. Check: is anything there that
you *didn't* say? (There shouldn't be.)

## C. Coming back (5 minutes, 6+ hours later)

1. Open MAYA's page. Before you say anything, she should recall where you left off and ask whether
   anything has changed — in your language.
2. Ask something that depends on last time ("What should I do about my parents?" / "Mera next step
   kya hai?") — her answer should use what you told her, without you repeating it.
3. Change your mind about something ("Actually I'm more interested in cybersecurity now"). After the
   session ends, *What she remembers* should show the new interest and drop or replace the old one;
   *My journey* should show the change.

## D. Control (2 minutes)

1. Delete one item (two taps) → it disappears and MAYA no longer brings it up.
2. Permissions → untick **Remember our conversations** → Save: it should say how many remembered
   items were deleted, and *What she remembers* should show "Memory is off".
