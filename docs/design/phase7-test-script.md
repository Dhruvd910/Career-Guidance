# Phase 7 hands-on test — the continuous mentor

About 30 minutes, over two sittings at least 6 hours apart. Use MAYA on the Pi as a student would. Tell Claude "done"
afterwards and it will read what MAYA recorded and check it.

Before you start, make sure **Edit my details** has your class and town, and that the **memory**
permission is on (Menu → **What MAYA remembers**). Without it, MAYA still uses your roadmap,
assessments and shortlist, but not your conversations. That's deliberate.

## A. First sitting: leave something open (8 minutes, by voice)

1. Talk about a choice you haven't made. For example: *"Mujhe PCM aur PCB mein confusion hai.
   Computer pasand hai, par ghar wale doctor chahte hain."* Don't decide. Say *"abhi decide nahi
   kar pa rahi/raha"*.
2. On a college's page (Menu → **Colleges** → search **manit**), tap **Save to my shortlist**. The
   button changes to **✓ On your shortlist (remove)**.
3. Or ask MAYA: *"IIITM Gwalior ko meri shortlist mein daal do."* She should add it. If the name
   could mean several colleges, she should ask **which one** instead of guessing.
4. End the session, or leave MAYA alone for 10 minutes: idle sessions close on their own, and she writes
   her notes then.

## B. What's on screen (6 minutes)

1. Home: under the greeting, a line **Where we are ›** shows the most important thing right now,
   with its reason. Tap it.
2. **Where we are** has these sections:
   - **Where we left off**: the open topic and its open questions. This appears only with the memory
     permission.
   - **What's next**: each item with *why*. Tap **Open** and it goes to the right screen (the
     roadmap, an exam, a college, an assessment).
   - **Working toward**: your focus, the roadmap's next step and your goals.
   - **How you've progressed**: skills that moved, roadmap %, assessments taken.
   - **Decided**: what you decided, and where that's recorded.
3. Tap **Not now** on one item. It disappears, and it should stay away for 14 days.
4. Menu → **My shortlist**. Each college shows its key facts (fees, hostel, medical, nearest
   station, NIRF), each with its source and freshness. **Compare them** opens the comparison.

## C. Second sitting: MAYA picks up (10 minutes, by voice)

Come back at least 6 hours later. If you come back sooner, she carries on without a recap.

1. MAYA's first words should recall where you left off: the open topic, plus at most two things
   worth raising, each with its reason. She shouldn't start from zero ("Hi, how can I help?").
2. Ask *"Aaj kya baat karein?"* She should offer one or two things from **What's next**, each with
   its reason, and let you choose.
3. Decide: *"PCM final hai, mujhe computer science karna hai."*
   - She should **offer** to make computer science your roadmap's focus and to set your stream,
     not do it on her own.
   - Check **Edit my details**: the stream hasn't changed yet.
4. Say *"Haan, focus bana do"*, then *"Stream bhi PCM kar do."* Now both change:
   - the roadmap has a new version (Roadmap → History)
   - **Edit my details** shows PCM
   - She should only say the roadmap changed if it actually did.
5. Say *"Ye abhi nahi"* about something she raises. She should accept it and not bring it up
   again this session.
6. Ask *"Meri shortlist mein kya hai?"* She should name your colleges and give key facts, with
   where each comes from.

## D. What was recorded (3 minutes)

Menu → **What MAYA remembers** → **Recent conversations**. Each session has two parts:
- The summary, which is the notes model's account of the conversation.
- **What happened**, taken from the modules themselves: roadmap versions made, steps ticked,
  assessments taken, colleges shortlisted. Nothing in this part should be something you didn't do.

## Not testable by hand in one day

The six-month reassessment, and a shortlisted college's facts changing between sessions, need time
to pass. Both were rehearsed on the test database with the clock moved forward (see
`16-phase7-plan.md`, "As built"). Moving up a class is testable: change your class in **Edit my
details**, and a **Retake** item for any assessment you took appears in **What's next**.

## What to tell Claude

"done", plus anything that felt wrong, especially:
- MAYA nagging, or raising the same thing twice in one session
- MAYA changing something you didn't agree to
- a reason that didn't match what was on screen
