# Phase 5 hands-on test — the roadmap and progress

About 30 minutes. Use MAYA on the Pi as a student would. Tell Claude "done" afterwards and it will
read what MAYA did and check it. It helps to have taken the aptitude assessment at least once
(Phase 3); taking it twice shows a skill moving on *My progress*.

## A. Your first roadmap (5 minutes)

1. Menu → **My roadmap**. The first time, it's built from your class: the current stage is open at
   the top (*· now*), and later stages are closed below it. Without a focus career it says
   *No focus career yet — your roadmap covers the basics and exploring.*
2. Assessments you've already taken show as ✓, each with its month window (for example
   *Oct–Nov 2026*).
3. Tap the **Next step** card's **Open**. The step's page shows *Why*, *Done when*, *Things to try*
   (links show a QR code when tapped), and the buttons *Started / Done / Not yet*.

## B. Choosing a focus (5 minutes)

1. Menu → **Career directions** (or **Careers**) → open a career you like → **Make this my focus**.
   You land on the roadmap.
2. The top line says *Focus: <career>*. The current stage now has that career's skills in order:
   foundations first, then the skills that build on them, then projects.
3. Skills you already scored strongly on in an assessment are ✓. Open one: it says *from an
   assessment: <your result>*.
4. Tap **What changed** (at the top of the page). Version 2 says *You chose a new focus: …*, and
   **All versions** lists version 1 too.

## C. Changing it yourself (5 minutes)

1. **Your time** → *I have less (or more) time* → tap **1**. You land on *What changed*: some steps
   *Moved later*, with the reason *it doesn't fit in 1 hour a week*. Nothing exam-related is moved.
2. Back on the roadmap, **Something's hard** → **Maths**. A *Maths foundation* step appears before
   the steps that build on maths, and *What changed* says *Added because you said maths feels hard.*
3. Mark any step **Done** on its page. Its tick stays after the changes above: progress carries
   over between versions.

## D. Telling MAYA (10 minutes, by voice)

Say these in your own words and language. For the first three, MAYA should **tell you what would
change and ask first**. Only after you say yes should she say it's done; then *What changed* has a
new version.

1. *"I only have two hours a day."* She should say the roadmap gets about 2 hours a week, beside
   school and exam study, and some steps move later.
2. *"Maths mujhe mushkil lagta hai."* She should offer a maths foundation step. (If you did C.2,
   she may say nothing needs to change, which is also right.)
3. *"I no longer want <your focus>. I'm interested in cybersecurity."* After you agree: the focus
   is Cybersecurity, and *My progress* → *Careers and goals* says *Moved away from: <old focus>*.
   The old career's own skills are *Parked*, not deleted.
4. *"Mera next step kya hai?"* She should name the roadmap's next step and say why.
5. *"Main kitna improve hua hoon?"* She should quote your first and latest results (for example
   *2 of 5 → 5 of 5*) and call it an improvement only if it is one.
6. *"Maine <a step> poora kar liya."* She should name the step she ticked. Check that it's the one
   you said.

## E. My progress (5 minutes)

1. Menu → **My progress**. Each measured skill shows *First* and *Now* as bars, with the latest
   result and its date. *Improved* appears only for a real change.
2. *Done so far* counts milestones, projects and assessments taken. The note at the bottom says skill
   levels come only from assessments and practice papers. Ticking a step doesn't raise a skill.
3. Switch to **हिंदी** on the roadmap: step names and the reasons in *What changed* are in Hindi.
