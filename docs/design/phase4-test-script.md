# Phase 4 hands-on test — the career engine

About 25 minutes. Use MAYA on the Pi as a student would. Tell Claude "done" afterwards and it will
read what MAYA did and check it. Before you start, make sure **Edit my details** has a state (for
example Madhya Pradesh); the college answers use it.

## A. Exploring without any assessment (5 minutes)

1. Menu → **Careers**. Careers are grouped by area. *Technology* lists Software, AI & Data,
   Cybersecurity, Robotics and Design.
2. Tap **Cybersecurity** (new in this phase). Check that the page shows:
   - *Not assessed yet*
   - **How people get there**, with numbered steps: stream → exam → degree → work
   - the class 11–12 subjects
   - **Other routes in** (BCA, B.Sc CS)
   - **Skills it needs**, each marked *not measured yet*
   - a **learning path** that starts with the foundations, each step with something to try
   - **Colleges that offer it**, with a count for your state

   Tap a college: it opens that college's page.
3. Tap a **related career**: it opens that career.

## B. Choosing a stream (5 minutes)

1. Careers → **Choosing a stream? See what each one keeps open** (or menu → *Stream explorer*).
2. Tap **PCB**. Check three things:
   - Doctor stays open.
   - Computer Science stays open via BCA, with a note about maths.
   - Robotics closes and says it needs Mathematics.
3. Tap **Commerce**. AI & Data should show under *Opens if you add a subject (Mathematics)*.
4. Switch to **हिंदी**: the career names change to Hindi.

## C. Your own directions (5 minutes; needs Phase 3's *What you enjoy* done)

1. Menu → **Career directions**. Careers are grouped by area. Each row shows its band, a reason
   and *Usually: <degree> · N colleges in <your state>*.
2. Open a *Strong* one. Its skills show your own results wherever you took a check (for example
   the coding check), and *to work on* appears only where something was measured.

## D. Asking MAYA (10 minutes, by voice)

Ask, in your own words and language:

1. *"PCB lu toh kya options khule rahenge?"* She should name careers that stay open and say what
   closes (engineering needs maths).
2. *"Agar mujhe AI karna hai toh mere aas paas kaunse colleges hain?"* She should name real
   colleges in your state, and say fees and hostels aren't available yet.
3. *"AI engineer banne ke liye kya kya seekhna padega?"* She should give skills to learn in order
   and suggest a first project.
4. *"Lawyer kaise bante hain?"* She should describe BA LLB through CLAT/AILET, or LLB after
   graduation, then the AIBE.
5. If MAYA remembers an interest of yours (Phase 2), check *Career directions* or that career's
   page for *"You told MAYA you're interested in …"*.
